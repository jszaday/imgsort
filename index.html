Sorting Complete!

Nothing is moved into category folders and nothing is deleted. Each checked item is moved once into a single hidden per-run store, and every category you keep it in gets a lightweight navigational reference (symlink, or a .lnk shortcut on Windows) pointing back at the store. Trash items are interned but left unreferenced — recoverable from the store, never removed. Uncheck a box to drop that one reference. The script updates live.
